Diagnostics, straight through to repair

We are proud to offer fully inclusive solutions to all vehicle related issues and repair requirements. Comprising a team of experienced mechanics, engineers, auto locksmiths and auto electricians, the team manage the job from initial diagnosis, through to full repair, regardless of the job requirement. Vehicle diagnostics & problem solving

A large percentage of work flow comes from other garages and vehicle auctions, with the vehicles predominantly in a ‘non-runner’ state, or experiencing recurring undiagnosed faults. Our team are highly experienced in fault finding, and have built our reputation based on accurate results, leading to streamlined repairs.

Software updates & programming

Software updates and module programming are becoming an increasingly high demand service with modern vehicles. Our team use dealer tools and/or dealer level advanced tools for coding procedures, across most makes and models.*

*Particular focus on Land Rover, Jaguar, Bentley, VW, Audi, Skoda, Seat, Ford and BMW vehicles

Advanced level of repairs

Further to the diagnosis, and subject to quote approval, the team will then move on to the required repair. High quality and/or OEM parts are used across all makes. A dedicated engineer is assigned to each job, based on the job requirement and technician specialism to ensure a high quality of service at all times.

50+ years experience

Merging old school mechanical engineering, with modern day computer programming and high end diagnostics, to create an all encompassing team of automotive engineers, and an outstanding service for our clients.
